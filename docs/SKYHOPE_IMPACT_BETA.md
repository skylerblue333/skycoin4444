# SkyHope Impact Beta

SkyHope is an engineering-beta impact workspace that connects existing SKYCOIN4444 learning, HopeAI, gaming, creator, and charity-policy components.

## What is implemented

- a versioned server-side impact boundary;
- four authored cross-product impact mission templates;
- a server-backed finance-readiness evaluator that reuses the existing fail-closed charity-only policy core;
- direct paths from SkySchool to HopeAI, practice games, and the impact workspace;
- first-class School and Impact navigation entries;
- explicit evidence language for every mission.

## Finance boundary

The current beta does **not** execute donations, custody, withdrawals, token settlement, redeemable crypto rewards, or real-money wagers.

The finance-readiness evaluator can only determine whether an action would be eligible for a future external-provider handoff. It still requires a verified charity beneficiary, approved provider, legal review, region eligibility, and—where applicable—age gating and a regulated gaming provider.

SKYCOIN4444 does not currently verify charities, hold charity funds, broadcast blockchain transactions, or certify compliance.

## Product rule

Learning progress, HopeAI plans, game scores, XP, Sparks, demo credits, creator drafts, and mission completion are useful beta evidence, but none of them are proof that money moved or that a real-world impact outcome occurred.
