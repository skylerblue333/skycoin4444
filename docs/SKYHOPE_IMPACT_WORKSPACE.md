# SkyHope Impact Workspace — Engineering Beta

## Purpose

SkyHope turns the old charity surface into an evidence-first impact planning workspace instead of presenting simulated donations, invented donor totals, fake partner verification, or unsupported on-chain claims.

The workspace connects four existing product areas:

- **SkyHope** — campaign and impact planning.
- **HopeAI** — a generated planning brief that the user can take to the HopeAI workspace for critique.
- **SkySchool** — an authored responsible-giving and impact-measurement learning track.
- **Impact Play Lab** — deterministic game-only allocation practice with no cash or token value.
- **Social** — community discovery and discussion remain separate from any claim that a cause or partner is verified.

## What works in this beta

- Deterministic campaign-plan generation with validated title, theme, beneficiary goal, objective, and evidence metric.
- Four evidence checkpoints at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of the planning target.
- A deterministic HopeAI coaching brief that explicitly blocks unsupported verification/payment/blockchain claims.
- Device-local saved drafts.
- A primary SkyHope navigation entry and voice-navigation keyword.
- A SkySchool course covering needs discovery, evidence, delivery, safeguards, and transparent reporting.
- A deterministic Impact Play Lab with a no-value practice score.

## Current boundary

This is an engineering-beta planning and learning surface.

It **does not**:

- execute donations or payments;
- hold custody;
- verify beneficiaries or charities;
- verify external partners;
- broadcast blockchain transactions;
- create on-chain proofs;
- provide a durable server-side campaign store;
- predict real-world charitable impact from a game score.

Before any live donation flow is enabled, the platform still needs a durable campaign/beneficiary data model, approved payment-provider integration, authorization, receipts/refunds, fraud controls, reconciliation, legal/region review, and evidence that the beneficiary was actually verified.

## Product principle

The charity experience should never manufacture social proof. If the platform cannot prove a donor total, partner relationship, beneficiary verification, transaction, or outcome, the interface must say so rather than filling the gap with demo numbers.
