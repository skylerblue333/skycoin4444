# SkyHope Impact Workspace — Engineering Beta

The canonical `/charity` surface is a planning and impact-evidence workspace, not a live donation processor.

## Delivered in this increment

- authored cause tracks with direct paths into HopeAI, SkySchool, Social, and Gaming;
- deterministic campaign planning with bounded inputs and milestone prompts;
- device-local campaign draft saving;
- volunteer-capacity planning;
- impact-evidence readiness scoring across baseline, consent, metrics, cadence, and privacy;
- explicit finance/provider gate status rather than simulated donation success;
- release tests that reject fake DAO, fake donor ranking, and unsupported on-chain claims.

## Finance and charity boundary

SKYCOIN4444 does not claim that this workspace verifies charities, holds funds, settles donations, broadcasts blockchain transactions, provides tax receipts, establishes tax deductibility, or certifies impact.

Any future real-value donation path requires a separately verified beneficiary, approved external provider, legal review, allowed region, transaction/receipt evidence, privacy controls, and provider-specific operational testing. The existing charity-finance policy remains an authorization/planning boundary, not an execution provider.

## HopeAI boundary

HopeAI may assist with campaign briefs, volunteer plans, outreach drafts, evidence checklists, and summaries of user-provided material. It must not claim a charity is verified or that money moved unless a separately connected and verified provider supplies that evidence.
